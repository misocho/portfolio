## Engineering Approach

### Reliability before complexity

I prioritize clear application boundaries, explicit business rules, predictable error handling, and testable components. I aim to solve the actual engineering problem before introducing additional infrastructure or abstractions.

### AI with clear boundaries

I treat AI as a capability that can help interpret, classify, and extract information. For important business operations, I favor validation, deterministic rules, and appropriate human oversight around AI-generated outputs.

### Performance grounded in measurement

I value identifying bottlenecks and measuring the impact of changes rather than optimizing based on assumptions. My experience with backend processing optimization has reinforced the importance of evaluating improvements against real workloads.

### Data integrity and maintainability

I focus on designing understandable data models, validating inputs, preserving consistent application state, and building systems that remain manageable as requirements evolve.

### Software that solves operational problems

I am interested in the systems behind useful products: integrations, data pipelines, APIs, and workflows that help people make decisions and complete work more effectively.
